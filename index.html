diff-voyager

before / after migration diff

A tool for comparing a website before and after a migration, so you can see what changed, what broke and what quietly got lost along the way.

What it looks at

Visuals

Side-by-side and overlay comparison of pages, so layout shifts and missing elements are easy to spot.

SEO

Titles, meta tags, canonicals, headings, redirects and indexing signals, checked before against after.

Communication

Copy, calls to action, links and messaging, so the story your site tells survives the move.

The idea

Before

Capture the old site as the reference.

After

Capture the migrated site and diff it against that reference.
